# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 13 Nov 2026*

Pacifyco ("**we**", "**us**") runs the Pacifyco app, which helps families in Bengaluru find and book verified babysitters ("**sitters**"). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, who can see it, how long we keep it and the rights you have. It applies to parents, sitters and anyone else who uses the app.

## 1. Who we are

Pacifyco, Bengaluru. You can reach us at founders@pacifyco.in or +91 8848873841. Our Grievance Officer's details are in our Terms of Use.

## 2. What we collect

**Parents:** your name, email, mobile number, home address and pin code, and your relationship to your children; each child's name and date of birth, and any allergies, medical notes and routines you choose to share; each child's birth certificate (deleted once we have checked it); your emergency contact's name, relationship and number; and a record of the consents and terms you accept (the date and time, your IP address and your device's browser details).

**Sitters:** your name, email, mobile number, home address and pin code, date of birth, photo, and what you tell us about your experience, the ages you care for, the languages you speak and first aid; your government ID, college or work ID, police clearance certificate and first-aid certificate; your references' names, phone numbers and how they know you; your psychology screening answers; any health disclosure you choose to make; your payout details (bank account or UPI ID); your availability; and a record of the consents and terms you accept.

**Everyone:** your sign-in details, your bookings, messages and payment references as those features become available, and the reviews you give and receive.

## 3. Why we use it

- To create and run your account, and to sign you in.
- To verify families and sitters before anyone can be booked.
- To show parents suitable sitters and to arrange bookings.
- To take payments and pay sitters.
- To keep children, families and sitters safe, and to handle emergencies, incidents and complaints.
- To meet our legal obligations.
- To send offers and updates, only if you have said yes. You can change your mind at any time, and saying no changes nothing about your service.

## 4. Children's data

We collect a child's data only from their parent or legal guardian, and only after the parent has given their consent in the app. We use it only to match and brief a sitter and to keep the child safe. We never use a child's data for advertising, tracking or profiling. A birth certificate is deleted as soon as we have checked it; we keep only the result, who checked it and when.

## 5. Who can see it

- **The Pacifyco team**, only as much as they need for their work. Every time a team member opens a document, it is recorded.
- **The sitter you book** sees your child's care details for that booking. Sitters never see your documents.
- **Parents** see a live sitter's name, photo, profile, the checks she has passed and her reviews. Parents never see a sitter's address, phone number, pay, documents or health disclosure.
- **The companies that run the app for us**, under contract and only to provide their service: database, sign-in and file storage (Supabase, in Mumbai), hosting (Render and Cloudflare), email (Resend) and error reports that contain no personal data (Sentry).
- **The police, courts and authorities**, when the law requires it, or to protect a child or anyone else in danger.

We never sell your data.

## 6. Where it is stored

Your account details and documents are stored in India (Mumbai). Some of the companies above may handle limited data outside India to do their job, for example your email address when we send you an email.

## 7. How we protect it

Documents are kept in private storage and are opened only through links that expire within minutes. Health disclosures and bank details are encrypted. Only the Pacifyco team can view documents, and every view is recorded. The app can't be read directly from a browser: everything goes through our own checks.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Birth certificates are deleted once checked. When your account is closed, we delete or anonymise your data, except records the law requires us to keep (for example, payment records and safety incidents), which we keep only for as long as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent, and you can nominate someone to act for you. Use Help → Raise a complaint in the app (choose "data request"), or email founders@pacifyco.in. If you withdraw a consent we need to provide the service, we may not be able to keep your account open. If you are not happy with our answer, you can contact our Grievance Officer, and then the Data Protection Board of India.

## 10. Cookies and similar storage

We do not use advertising cookies or trackers. The app keeps your sign-in session and your light or dark theme choice in your browser, so it works as you expect.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
