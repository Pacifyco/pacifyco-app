# Before you book: a few things to know

## Getting started

- **Be at home for the first 2 bookings** with a new sitter. It helps your child settle in and gives you both time to get to know each other.
- **Tell your sitter about your child:** routines, food, nap times, allergies, health concerns and anything that comforts them. Add it to your child’s profile in the app, so it’s there every time.
- **Check it’s the right sitter at the door.** Make sure they match the photo and name in the app before giving them your check-in code.

## Your home

- **Our sitters won’t stay if the only adult at home is a man.** Please plan bookings around this. If a sitter has to decline for this reason, the full booking is charged.
- **Tell us who’s at home:** other adults, household staff, and any pets.
- **Cameras are fine,** but let the sitter know where they are. Never place one in a bathroom or anywhere private.
- **Water, washroom and food.** Please give your sitter drinking water and a clean washroom, and offer them food on all-day bookings.
- **Keep it safe:** medicines, sharp objects, balconies and windows secured. No alcohol, smoking or drugs around the sitter during a booking.

## Treating your sitter well

- **Your sitter is there only to care for your child.** Please don’t ask them to cook, clean, do house chores or run errands.
- **No photos or videos** of your sitter without their consent.
- **Outings and travel.** If you need your sitter to step out or travel with you and your child, let them know beforehand.
- **Potty training and diaper changes** are for you and your sitter to agree on before the booking. Your sitter is free to say no.
- **Medicine** only if you’ve written the name, dose and time in the app.
- **Respect, always.** Any harassment, abuse or mistreatment of a sitter will lead to account closure and legal action.

## Payments

- **Let Pacifyco handle all payments.** Never pay your sitter directly. Tips aren’t expected; if you’d like to give one, it’s entirely your choice.
- **Prices are fixed** and can’t be negotiated.
- **Cancel at least 3 hours before** for a full refund. If no one is home or the booking can’t go ahead because of you, the full booking is charged.

## During the booking

- **Stay reachable** by phone, and be home by the booked end time. If you’re running late, your sitter can only stay on if they’re available and you approve the overtime in the app.

## If something goes wrong

- **In an emergency:** call **112**, then press the Emergency button in the app.
- **Report** anything that worried you within 24 hours. We reply within 24 hours.
