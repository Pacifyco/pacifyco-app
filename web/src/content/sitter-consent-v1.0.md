## Your safety comes first

- **If the only adult at home is a man,** we don’t recommend going in. You can decline the booking on the spot. Just tell us through the app. It won’t count as a strike, and you’ll still receive ₹100.
- **Something feels off?** You can leave once the child is safe with an adult. Press the Emergency button, and we’ll take it from there.
- **Share your location** with a friend or family member when you head to a booking, and plan your ride home, especially for late finishes.
- **Keep it on the app.** You don’t need to share your personal number or social media handles with families. You also don’t have to answer personal questions you’re uncomfortable with.

## Money and gifts

- **Tips are entirely the parent’s choice.** Please never ask a family for tips, cash or extra money. If you need to stay longer, request overtime in the app.
- **All payments go through Pacifyco.** Don’t accept payment from families directly.

## Respecting the family’s home

- **Never take anything** that isn’t clearly given to you. Theft means permanent removal and a police complaint.
- **There may be cameras.** Families tell you where they are, and cameras are never allowed in bathrooms.

## Showing up

- **Be on time.** Parents plan their time around you. If you’re running late, tell the parent and us through the app straight away. If you’re more than 15 minutes late without telling us, you’re only paid from when you check in, and the parent can cancel the booking. A cancelled booking counts as a no-show and a strike.
- **Feeling unwell?** Tell us at least 8 hours before so we can find a replacement.

## Caring for the child

- **Potty training and diaper changes** are something you and the parent agree on before the booking. You’re free to say no. If you agree, keep the bathroom door open and follow the parent’s routine.
- **Never leave the child alone,** and never take them outside without the parent’s permission in the app.
- **Medicine** only if the parent has written the instructions in the app.
- **No photos or videos** of the child, the home or the family.

## If something goes wrong

- **In an emergency:** make the child safe, call **112**, then press the Emergency button.
- **Report** anything that worried you within 24 hours. We reply within 24 hours.
