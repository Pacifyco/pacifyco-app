## **1. Joining Pacifyco**

1.1 You must be **18 or older**, legally allowed to work in India, and have no record of offences involving children, violence, sexual offences, drugs or dishonesty.

1.2 **Checks.** Every sitter goes through an ID check, police verification, a reference check and a psychology test. An admin then approves your profile. You consent to these checks at sign-up, and to repeat checks from time to time.

1.3 **Psychology test.** We may decline your application based on the results of the psychology test or any other check. We will tell you if you are not approved.

1.4 **Documents we keep.** We keep secure digital copies of your government ID, college or work ID and police verification certificate. Only Pacifyco admins can see them, and every view is logged. We do not keep your original documents.

1.5 **Bank details.** To pay you, we collect your bank account or UPI details. We store them securely, use them only to pay you, and share them only with our payment provider.

1.6 **Health.** Before you join, and whenever anything changes, you must tell us about any serious or long-term health condition, or any allergy (for example, to pets, dust or foods), that could affect your ability to care for a child safely. We use this only to match you safely and keep it private.

1.7 **Fake documents.** If you give false documents or information, including about your age, we will remove you permanently, withhold any amounts linked to that fraud, and report it to the police.

1.8 You must tell us within **24 hours** if you are arrested, charged or investigated for any offence, or if any information you gave us changes.

## **2. Bookings and pay**

2.1 **Your slots.** You choose when you are available. Interviews and bookings are placed only in the slots you mark as free, so keep your availability up to date. A booking placed in a slot you marked free counts as accepted.

2.2 **Interviews.** Before working with a family for the first time, you will have a short interview with the parent, run by Pacifyco. You can work with that family only if the interview is passed.

2.3 **Your rate.** You set your hourly rate between **₹80 and ₹120**. You are paid per hour, for each child you look after. Families pay Pacifyco, and Pacifyco keeps the difference between what the family pays and your pay. Bookings are at least 2 hours, and time is rounded up to the next half hour. Travel is not paid.

2.4 **Check-in and check-out.** When you arrive, check in on the app using the parent's 4-digit check-in code. Before you leave, check out using the parent's 4-digit check-out code, which the parent gives you once they are back with the child. Both record your location. You are paid from check-in to check-out, up to the booked end time plus any overtime the parent has approved (see 2.5).

2.5 **Overtime.** If you need to stay longer (including when a parent is running late), request the extra time in the app **before** you work it. Overtime is paid only once the parent approves it. You never have to stay past the booked time, but you must never leave a child alone.

2.6 **Payouts.** We pay your earnings to your bank or UPI account within **12 hours** of check-out (or of overtime approval). If a booking has an open incident report, the payout for that booking is held until the incident is resolved.

2.7 **Payments only through Pacifyco.** Pay for booked time and overtime is made only through Pacifyco. Do not ask for or accept payment from a family for a booking, and do not arrange babysitting with a family you met through us outside the app while you are on Pacifyco.

2.8 **Tips.** A tip is entirely the parent's choice. You must never ask for, expect or pressure a family for tips, cash or extra money. If extra work is needed, request it as overtime in the app.

2.9 **Gifts.** Any gift or item a family chooses to give you is a private matter between you and the family. Pacifyco is not involved and is not responsible for it. Never ask for gifts, and take an item only if a parent has clearly given it to you.

2.10 You are responsible for your own income tax. We deduct only the taxes the law requires us to deduct.

3.2 **Illness.** If you have a fever, flu or anything that could spread to a child, do not come to the booking. Tell us and the parent **at least 8 hours** before the start so a replacement can be found. If you fall ill suddenly with less notice, tell us immediately; we may cancel the strike if you send a doctor's note.

3.3 We do not charge cancellation fees or fines. The only consequences of cancelling are losing pay for that booking and strikes.

## **4. During a booking**

4.1 **You must never:**

- abuse or harm a child in any way (see 4.2);

- arrive under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or use them during a booking;

- leave the child alone, or with anyone else;

- leave the home during the booking, even briefly;

- take the child outside the home without the parent's permission in the app;

- invite anyone into the home;

- give medicine or food against the parent's instructions; or

- take photos or videos of the child, the home or the parents, or post anything about them, without the parent's consent.

4.2 **No abuse, ever.** Child abuse in any form is strictly forbidden. This includes physical abuse (hitting, slapping, pinching, shaking or rough handling), emotional or verbal abuse (shouting at, threatening, humiliating or frightening a child), sexual abuse (any sexual touch, act, talk or material) and neglect (ignoring the child's needs or withholding food, water or care). Child abuse is a serious crime under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, the Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015 and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. If abuse is reported, your account is suspended at once. If the evidence supports the report, you will be removed permanently and Pacifyco will report the matter to the police and share relevant records with the authorities.

4.3 **Allergies and medical notes.** Read the child's profile before each booking and follow its allergy and medical notes exactly.

4.4 **Child injured or unwell.** Give basic first aid, call **112** if the situation is serious, then call the parent and press the Emergency button. **Basic first aid** means simple, immediate steps such as cleaning and covering a small cut, pressing on a wound to slow bleeding, applying a cold compress to a bump, and keeping the child calm and in a safe position. It does **not** include giving any medicine unless the parent has told you to. Call 112 straight away for heavy bleeding, difficulty breathing, choking that does not clear, unconsciousness, a head injury, a suspected broken bone or an allergic reaction. If you are found to have caused harm through carelessness or on purpose, you will be removed and may be held legally responsible.

4.5 **Theft.** Taking anything from a family's home without permission is theft. If theft is reported, your account is suspended and that booking's payout is held while we investigate. If it is proven, you will be removed permanently, must return or pay for what was taken, and we will report it to the police.

4.6 **Home cameras.** Families may use cameras in their home. The family must tell you before the booking if cameras are on and where they are. By accepting the booking, you consent to being recorded in those areas. Cameras are not allowed in bathrooms or anywhere you change. Footage belongs to the family, but if there is an incident you can ask us to review the relevant footage.

4.7 **Parent not home.** If no one is home when you arrive, call the parent and press the Report button; our team will try to reach them too. If no parent responds within **20 minutes**, you may leave, and we pay you ₹100. If a child is there without an adult, do not leave: press the Emergency button and wait for our team.

4.8 **Child under 18 months.** Pacifyco only takes bookings for children aged 18 months and above. If the child turns out to be younger than 18 months, you are not required to care for them. Press Report and tell Pacifyco immediately. You may leave as soon as a parent or another adult is with the child, but never leave a young child alone. You will receive ₹100 and it will not count as a strike.

4.9 **Food and washroom.** The family will give you drinking water and access to a clean washroom during every booking. For bookings of 4 hours or more, you may bring your own food and eat when the child is safely settled.

4.10 **Reporting abuse by others.** If you see or suspect that a child is being abused by anyone, tell us immediately. Under the POCSO Act, anyone who knows of or suspects sexual abuse of a child must report it to the police. We will support you.
